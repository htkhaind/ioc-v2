PRIVACY POLICY FOR IOC-EDU

Last updated: [Date, e.g., October 27, 2026]

This Privacy Policy describes how VNPT Information Technology Company/Digital Education Solutions Center ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the IOC-EDU mobile application (the "App").

IOC-EDU is a dedicated platform designed to support leaders, officials, and specialists at educational management agencies and institutions in monitoring, exploiting, and analyzing educational data through visual charts and reports.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect
Because IOC-EDU is an internal/authorized management tool, we only collect minimal information necessary to authenticate users and provide role-based data access:

Personal and Account Information: When your account is created or managed, we may process your full name, official email address, phone number, job title, affiliated educational agency/institution, and login credentials.

Usage and Technical Data: We may automatically collect basic technical information about your device (such as device model, operating system version, IP address, and crash logs) to ensure system security, maintain session stability, and improve app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information
The information we collect is strictly used for the following purposes:

Authentication and Access Control: To verify your identity and grant appropriate permissions to view specific Dashboards, statistical reports, and school databases within your management scope.

Account Management: To allow you to view and update your personal profile and customize security settings (e.g., changing passwords, enabling biometric login if applicable).

System Maintenance & Security: To monitor app stability, prevent unauthorized access, and protect sensitive educational statistics.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared in the following limited circumstances:

Within the Authorized Educational System: With governing educational bodies or system administrators responsible for managing the IOC-EDU platform.

Service Providers: With trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting services) who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

Legal Requirements: When required by law or government regulations.

4. Data Security
We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures (including data encryption in transit via HTTPS, secure authentication protocols, and strict role-based access control) to protect your personal information and educational data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion
We retain your personal account information for as long as your account remains active and authorized by your educational agency.

Account Deactivation/Deletion: Because user accounts are provisioned based on official roles within educational agencies, if you wish to delete your account or request the removal of your personal data, please contact your system administrator or reach out to us directly at [Your Support Email]. We will process your request in accordance with applicable regulations and internal data governance policies.

6. Children’s Privacy
IOC-EDU is strictly intended for adult professionals, educational administrators, and authorized staff. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 through this App.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
